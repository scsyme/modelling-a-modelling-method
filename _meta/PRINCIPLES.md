# Learning and publication principles

This is an independent SQL-based learning experiment inspired by Jessica Talisman’s *Ontology Pipeline: A Framework for Knowledge Engineering* (Technics Publications, 2026). It is not an official implementation or endorsed companion.

- Link to the [Studio](https://jesstalisman-ia.github.io/ontology-pipeline/) and identify the relevant stage for claims about the method.
- Respect the [Studio licence](https://jesstalisman-ia.github.io/ontology-pipeline/LICENSE.txt). Do not redistribute its code, text, checklists, example datasets or their exports without permission. Do not use the work for AI training.
- Publish original questions, independently written code, synthetic counterexamples and our own interpretations; do not recreate the book or Studio as a substitute for either.
- Distinguish source evidence, interpretation, modelling decisions and hypothetical test data. Missing evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Check third-party vocabulary licences separately. Attribution alone does not grant permission. Seek written permission when reuse is uncertain.
- Keep experiments small, falsifiable and reproducible. A passing query checks our model, not the correctness or completeness of the original methodology.
- Before publication, review the diff for provenance, rights and unintended disclosure. No blanket licence for third-party material is implied.

Reviewed against the public Studio licence on 2026-11-24. This is a project policy, not legal advice.
